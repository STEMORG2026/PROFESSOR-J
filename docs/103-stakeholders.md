**Who are the people and systems touched by this project?**

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs | Influence |
|-------------|------|-------|-----------|
| Sajan (Principal Architect) | Owner/decider | A general-purpose AI platform that succeeds JARVIS | High — decides scope, governance, architecture |
| STEM students | Primary user | Socratic tutoring, grounded facts, exam prep | Medium — product shaping |
| Researchers / self-learners | User | Paper ingestion, citation provenance, sandboxed verification | Medium |
| Educators / content creators | User | Curriculum-aligned content, quiz banks | Medium |
| General personal users | User | Unified chat/file/code/workspace assistance | Medium |
| LearningHubSTEM | Knowledge source (peer repo) | Canonical exports consumed via adapter | Contract — never modified |
| JARVIS | Predecessor (peer repo) | Pattern source; stays independent | Pattern-level only |
| AI coding agents | Contributor | Clear governance, standards, verification commands | High for workflow |

**Who decides?** Sajan decides. Agents propose; the human owner approves (workspace
principle: AI output needs a human).

**Whose problem is being solved?** The learner's (getting taught, not just answered), the
researcher's (verified knowledge work), and the general user's (one capable assistant).

**Who is most affected when the project fails to deliver?** The learners and researchers
who would rely on grounded, safe, Socratic AI assistance — and Sajan, who carries the
vision forward from JARVIS.
