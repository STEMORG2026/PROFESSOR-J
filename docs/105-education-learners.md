**Who are the learners: backgrounds, levels, ages, prior knowledge?**

STEM students at SEE/NEB/A-levels and undergraduate levels (Physics, Math, CS); independent
researchers; educators; self-learners. Prior knowledge varies from beginner (no formal
prereqs) to advanced (research-grade math).

**Prerequisites assumed before starting, and how they are verified.**

For tutoring paths, prerequisite mastery is verified via the LearningHubSTEM
prerequisite graph and short diagnostic probes (EvaluatorAgent). The system recommends
mastering prerequisites (e.g. `lhs:phys.mass`, `lhs:phys.acceleration`) before advanced
topics (`lhs:phys.force`).

**Contexts of learning.**

Classroom support, exam preparation, self-study, and online tutoring. Time available per
week varies; sessions are short (minutes) to long (research dives).

**Diversity and accessibility requirements.**

WCAG-minded UI, keyboard navigation, screen-reader basics, language-level adaptation
(plain-English explanations for beginners; formal for advanced), LaTeX rendering for
math, alternative media for content (text + audio via voice classroom).

**Learner motivation, and what success looks like from the learner's own view.**

Learners want to genuinely understand and pass exams / finish research. Success from their
view: they solve problems independently, can explain the reasoning, and trust the answers
as grounded and cited.
